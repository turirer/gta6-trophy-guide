Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough is enough. There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission (PowerPyx). Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep trophy sessions cheat-free (PowerPyx, GTABase).

  1. Step 1 — Before you start & Chapter 1

    Do Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1: it is the first meditation spot for Positive Mental Attitude, the one trophy that may be missable, and it is gone after the chapter (PowerPyx). Scan vehicles and study animals as you travel — the 100% checklist counts them later.

    Covers: the opening story trophies, Baited My Tackle, Positive Mental Attitude

  2. Step 2 — Play the story, Chapters 1–5 60–80 hours

    Finish all five chapters and the final mission. Take optional missions as they appear — the bank vaults open with an optional Chapter 4 mission — and start the stranger strands as they unlock. All 12 story trophies unlock automatically and cannot be missed (PowerPyx).

    Covers: 12 story trophies + the final-mission gold

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & side quests about 10 hours

    Finish the mission strands for strangers. Eight counting strands earn Strangers & Freaks — Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count; Moses, Tyrone, Yasmani, Andy and Billy do not (GTA Zone). The five character strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) each carry their own trophy, and Bret’s hunting quests are Jason-only.

    Covers: Strangers & Freaks, the five character-strand trophies, Fair Game

  4. Step 4 — After the credits: the 100% checklist about 100 hours

    Open the checklist at Progress → Stats → 100% Completion. Everything you did during the story already counts and nothing on it is missable (GTABase). It wants 70 of 80 challenges, 300 stunts, all 42 shooting-range golds, all 42 drone races, 8 bank vaults, all 39 illegal operations, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals and 50 of 66 weapons (PowerPyx). Most of it falls out of the other trophies.

    Covers: A Long Road and most activity, collectible and robbery trophies

  5. Step 5 — Clean up the one-offs, then the platinum

    Mop up the free-roam one-offs (the jet at VCIA, the tank at Mile Zero, the partner robberies), the money and property trophies, and the five trophies whose triggers are still unconfirmed at launch. When the last one pops, Vice Got a Price! follows.

    Covers: the remaining trophies, then the platinum

Missable trophies

Potentially 1 of 55. PowerPyx and GTABase flag only Positive Mental Attitude as possibly missable: Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation may be required for it, and it disappears after Chapter 1 — whether it is required is still unconfirmed, so do it to be safe. Every story trophy can’t be missed, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable (GTABase, PowerPyx via GTA Zone).

Because there is no mission replay, keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. The checklist below flags the one trophy to watch.
